Skills and hooks now follow one naming convention: a skill is named after its task (`write-pr`) or ends on `-standard`; a hook ends on `-gate` (blocks) or `-check` (never blocks). "Write gates" are now "write checks". Skills (all keep the `lighthouse-` prefix in the installed name): `verify-pr` is `write-pr`, `handoff` is `write-handoff`, `instrument` is `place-instruction`, `spec-adversarial-review` is `review-spec`, `plan-adversarial-review` is `review-plan`, `worktree-audit` is `audit-worktrees`, `testing` is `test-standard`, `go-writing` is `go-standard`, `python-writing` is `python-standard`, `shell-gates` is `shell-standard`. Hooks: `main-commit-guard` is `main-commit-gate`, `commit-tests` is `commit-test-gate`, `usage-guard` is `usage-gate`, `go-check` is `go-lint-gate`, `ty-check` is `ty-gate`, `worktree-guard` is `worktree-check`, `worktree-linkcheck` is `md-link-check`, `code-language` is `code-language-check`, `bash-write-gates` is `bash-write-check`, `gofmt` is `gofmt-check`, `ruff-format` is `ruff-format-check`, `shellcheck` is `shell-lint-check`. `lighthouse init --upgrade` removes the installed copies under the old names. If you changed such an old copy locally, the upgrade refuses and writes nothing: revert your change, or run it with `--force` to remove the copy anyway. The `[usage_guard]` and `[test_gate]` sections in `lighthouse.toml` keep their names.
