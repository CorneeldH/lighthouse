Wait, but why?

Why does it stop me now? This map answers that. It follows one turn from the prompt to the stop, and shows which hook can step in at each moment, on which tool, from which pack, and what it does.

A rule sets the norm, a hook steps in. Each guardrail is the counterpart of a rule in AGENTS.md or a skill. The rule carries the norm beforehand; the hook steps in at the moment itself, with a warning or with a hard line, as the headers of main-commit-guard.sh and worktree-guard.sh explain.
