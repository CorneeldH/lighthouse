The map. Each room is a place in your repository, plus one on your machine. Each area in a room is somewhere lighthouse init writes, and the colour says whose it is: lighthouse's, shared with you, or yours. Click an area to see what it holds, how init writes it, the packages it comes from and what reads it.

What --upgrade does. lighthouse init --upgrade replaces what is lighthouse's and adds missing keys to lighthouse.toml. If you changed a part of lighthouse's locally, the whole run refuses and writes nothing until you pass --force. The dashed line shows the zone it touches.

  • lighthouse's: --upgrade replaces it
  • shared: only lighthouse's block or keys
  • yours: init leaves it alone
  • dashed line: the zone --upgrade touches
  • changed or deleted a file, block or hook of lighthouse's? --upgrade stops and writes nothing until you pass --force
