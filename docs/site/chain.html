Wait, but why?

The chain is the connections. Each skill does one job well on its own. This map shows what joins them: which step hands over to which, where a fresh session takes over, and which hooks check the work on the way.

Not every job takes the whole road. There are several routes through the chain. A small job skips the spec and the plan, a bug starts with debugging, and a known recipe runs one skill. route-issue picks the route, so a job pays only for the steps it needs.

Handoffs keep each phase clean. A handoff ends one session and starts the next with only what it needs: the issue, the spec or the plan. Long work does not drag its whole history along.
